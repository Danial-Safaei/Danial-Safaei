# Danial Safaei

**PhD Researcher — Safe Autonomy Research Group, WMG, University of Warwick**

I work on safety assurance and trustworthy evaluation for AI-enabled autonomous systems: how to tell whether a system's behaviour in simulation is evidence about its behaviour in the world, and how to make that claim defensible.

## Research

| Area | Focus |
|:--|:--|
| **Safety assurance for AI** | Arguing safety, reliability, and assurance claims with evidence when learned components operate in high-consequence settings |
| **Explainable evaluation** | Using interpretability methods as measurement instruments — comparing the evidence a model relies on, not only its outputs |
| **Synthetic-data fidelity** | Whether synthetic imagery is faithful to the decision-relevant structure of real data, so simulation results transfer |
| **Scenario-based testing** | Testing autonomous systems against the conditions that matter most for safety |

Doctoral research supervised by Dr Siddartha Khastgir and Dr Xingyu Zhao, in industrial partnership with Siemens Digital Industries Software.
